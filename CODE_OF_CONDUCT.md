# Code of Conduct

We want ayeo's issues, discussions, PRs and any other spaces we run to be welcoming and useful for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people working in a second language.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no, and say no politely yourself.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Flooding the project with low-effort or generated content after being asked to stop.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, email **hello@ayeo.io**. Reports are handled in confidence by the maintainers, and we'll let you know what we do about it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, close issues and PRs, and temporarily or permanently ban people who break this code. We'll usually start with a private warning unless the behaviour is serious.

This code applies in all ayeo project spaces, and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.
